# What's My Tab?

Imagine having 30+ tabs open, and all you can see on your browser are tiny website icons—many of which repeat—leaving you guessing which tab is the actual page you are looking for. What's My Tab? is an extension that helps you navigate through your numerous tabs using a side panel. Making your browser experience manageable and at ease.

## 📋 Features

- **Smart Search & Filtering:** Instantly filter your open tabs by title, URL, or toggle between *All*, *Favorites*, and *Grouped* views.
- **Domain-Based Grouping:** Automatically organizes your active tabs neatly into groups based on their websites.
- **Favorites Bar:** Pin up to 5 of your most important tabs to a quick-access bottom bar.
- **Dark/Light Mode:** Seamlessly switch between light and dark themes.

## ⚙️ Installation

1. Download the [WhatsMyTabExtension.zip](https://github.com/krismalayao/WhatsMyTab/blob/WhatsMyTab/WhatsMyTabExtension.zip) from this repository to your local machine.
2. Open Chrome and navigate to [chrome://extensions/](chrome://extensions/).
3. Enable Developer mode using the toggle switch in the top-right corner.
4. Click Load unpacked in the top-left corner.
5. Select the folder containing the files.
6. Open the Chrome Side Panel or click the extension icon to start managing your tabs.
